Fastest: soplex 3/6 (0.4243), mcf 4/44 (0.3851).
Trend: a low threshold (dead as soon as the counters lean dead) bypasses more blocks; it hurts
mcf, whose predictor already calls live blocks dead (2/2 is the slowest on both traces).
A high threshold with wider counters needs a longer run of dead evictions before a PC
counts as dead (more hysteresis), so mcf gets steadily faster with width. soplex is
nearly flat (0.420-0.4243): most of its blocks are dead, so any setting that bypasses
them works, and only the most aggressive setting loses.
